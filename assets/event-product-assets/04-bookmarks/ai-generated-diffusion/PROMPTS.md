# Diffusion artwork prompts

The five artworks were generated with the built-in image generation tool. Each was requested as one flat, front-facing, tall and narrow zodiac bookmark panel, with no mockup, lettering, logo, or border. The shared art direction was a refined tarot-card woodcut with hand-inked botanical or natural-history detail, fine etched lines, a screenprint feel, a deep matte midnight ground, antique gold and ivory, and quiet space in the lower fifth for final typesetting.

| Sign | Subject and palette |
|---|---|
| Aries | A graceful ram with curled horns stepping through flame-shaped wildflowers beneath an antique sun, with subtle star-chart dots; terracotta accents. |
| Cancer | A crescent moon reflected in a silver tidepool, with a finely drawn crab, seaweed, small stars, and celestial chart marks; weathered silver and dusky teal accents. |
| Leo | A regal resting lion among sunflowers and solar rays, with a benevolent antique sun and tiny stars; muted amber accents. |
| Scorpio | A scorpion around a deep red rose beneath a red crescent moon, still water, small stars, and botanical thorns; dark garnet accents. |
| Pisces | Two fish circling beneath a crescent moon, flowing seaweed, pearly shells, and bubbles as tiny constellations; dusky aqua accents. |

Each result was generated separately, then cropped to a 56 × 156 mm bleed canvas and typeset with an original line from the corresponding `outputs/zodiac-copy-collection/<sign>/matter.json` file.
